BUSINESS ANALYSIS · FINANCIAL OPERATIONS

Making refund evidence accessible inside the review workflow

Business need. After a payment refund is successfully processed, financial reviewers need a consistent transaction record available from the relevant workflow screens, rather than manually retrieving details from the payment provider.

Analysis approach. Break the request into the event trigger, required data, reusable document-generation capability, workflow touchpoints, and failure handling.

Refund response→Validate data→Populate template→Generate PDF→Display in workflow

Requirements covered

  • Generate the document only after a successful refund response.
  • Map transaction fields to a predefined HTML template.
  • Use a reusable backend helper rather than duplicate PDF logic.
  • Make the document accessible from pending and confirmed review states.
  • Handle incomplete data without producing misleading records.
